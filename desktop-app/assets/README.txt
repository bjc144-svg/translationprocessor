Park Evaluation Services - Company Assets

Place your company assets in this folder:

1. park_logo.png
   - Your company logo
   - Recommended size: 300x100 pixels
   - Format: PNG with transparent background
   - Used in: Document headers and certificates

2. park_signature.png
   - Park employee signature
   - Recommended size: 300x100 pixels
   - Format: PNG with transparent background
   - Used in: Park certification template

These files will be automatically inserted into processed documents.

If files are not present, placeholder text will be used instead.
